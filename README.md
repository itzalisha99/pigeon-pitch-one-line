One-line elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup, for the make.fun bounty "Pigeon Pitch, One Line".

## The pitch

> Our startup turns every statue in the city into a paid billboard. We already have the pigeons, the statues, and zero rent.

`main.py` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (122 characters, one line) after checking it against the bounty gate: 1–200 characters, not only white space, a single line.
